\noindent\textbf{Subject: Submission of Original Research Manuscript} \\
\textbf{Title:} \textit{Mechanized T-Duality and Frontier String Dynamics on $K3 \times T^2$: Closing the Loop via LeanFlow Stiff Solvers with Native Duality, Topological Data Analysis, and Lean 4 Model Verification}

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent Dear Editors of SciPost Physics,

We submit our original research manuscript for consideration as a Regular Research Article in \textit{SciPost Physics}.

A longstanding challenge in string theory and non-linear cosmology is that numerical solvers inevitably crash near self-dual radii ($R \approx \sqrt{\alpha'}$) or singular orbifold points due to artificial coordinate divergences. Furthermore, verifying that dynamical defect networks formed during cosmic phase transitions satisfy global quantum consistency constraints—such as Ramond-Ramond tadpole cancellation ($\sum Q_i = 0$)—has historically relied on manual, error-prone bookkeeping.

In this work, we present \textbf{LeanFlow}: a duality-aware neuro-symbolic framework coupling high-performance stiff numerical integration with discrete algebraic invariant checks in the Lean 4 interactive theorem prover. Following this submission's internal audit, we label every claim below by evidentiary tier (A: kernel-checked and adequate; B: exact arithmetic with a negative control; C: conjecture/interpretation; X: exploratory numerics), and we have revised several claims from the originally deposited version accordingly. Our manuscript couples numerical integration with post-hoc topological analysis (Numerical Integration $\to$ Topological Extraction via TDA Mapper $\to$ Lean discrete-consistency checking); Lean verifies discrete consistency checks algebraically, though these checks rest on hard-coded model assumptions (tier C), and delivers the following primary physical and methodological advances:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Native Duality Engine \& Algorithmic Section Condition}: LeanFlow embeds continuous Buscher symmetry locks ($R \leftrightarrow \alpha'/R$) and modular group $SL(2, \mathbb{Z})$ invariance into its numerical integration (SciPy's implicit Radau solver for the modulus flow). By co-simulating dual geometric frames (Type IIA $\leftrightarrow$ Type IIB), the solver circumvents coordinate singularities as the cosmological modulus relaxes from high energies toward the Fricke saddle ($\tau = i/\sqrt{12}$) and Kummer orbifold fixed point ($\tau = e^{i\pi/3}$) without metric breakdown (tier X; the numerical minimum reported in an earlier draft could not be traced to any committed simulation output and has been removed pending a reproducible source).

    \item \textbf{Closed-Loop Defect Verification}: Passing the stochastic Langevin dynamics of a multi-well landscape across thermal symmetry breaking ($T_c = 1.0$) into a Topological Data Analysis (TDA) Mapper 1-skeleton graph, we partition the network into string-theoretic equivalence classes (Attractor Vacua, Domain Walls, and Cosmic Strings). Node/edge counts and the graph's cycle rank $\beta_1$ vary between runs because the Mapper stage is not seeded, so we report this construction as tier X exploratory numerics rather than quote a single fixed count; $\beta_1$ here denotes the cycle rank of the Mapper graph, not a persistent-homology invariant. As a discrete algebraic consistency check (tier C), Lean 4 verifies that, given the hard-coded per-class charge assignments, the mapped equivalence classes satisfy Ramond-Ramond tadpole cancellation ($\sum Q_i = 0$); this checks internal consistency of the labeling scheme, not an independent physical derivation.

    \item \textbf{Frontier Non-Perturbative Dynamics}: We apply the pipeline to three core open problems in string theory:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \emph{Swampland Distance Conjecture}: Moduli geodesic flows on $K3 \times T^2$ are computed, with $H_0$ barcodes tracking the collapse of the Kaluza-Klein mass tower ($M \le M_0 e^{-\alpha \Delta d}$), where $\alpha = \numreconcile{1/\sqrt{2}}{alpha}$ is an imposed, hard-coded model input rather than a fitted or measured quantity, and the committed run's bound check carried 1.5$\times$ slack rather than a strict tolerance (tier X).
        \item \emph{Sen's Tachyon Condensation}: Non-linear brane-antibrane roll-down is simulated, extracting the localized BPS soliton via TDA Mapper. As a discrete algebraic consistency check (tier C, not an independent derivation), Lean 4 checks Grothendieck group charge conservation $[E] - [F] = [D_{\text{defect}}]$ given the modeled bundle classes assigned.
        \item \emph{Vacuum Decay in the Flux Landscape}: We explore vacuum decay using flat-space bounce dynamics solved via shooting methods; gravity is omitted, so this is not a Coleman--De Luccia bounce in the strict sense. The central charge $c(N) = 100(N+1)$ is a hand-chosen exploratory toy model (tier C, we conjecture it as a qualitative proxy), and Lean 4 checks monotonicity of this hand-picked formula rather than deriving a holographic $c$-theorem. Full Coleman--De Luccia dynamics with gravity is left to future work.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Cosmological Stability \& Energy Conditions}: We solve the non-linear Poisson boundary value problem for Symmetron screening, obtaining a screening factor $\Delta R/R \approx \numreconcile{1.18\times 10^{-4}}{screening\_factor}$ from the committed simulation output (tier X, pending validation); comparison against Cassini solar-system bounds is in progress and no numerical Cassini parameter is reported here, as none exists yet in our simulation output. Separately, we formalize the Weak Energy Condition $w(t) \ge -1$ as a runtime boundary condition (tier C): when the target-space imaginary modulus $\tau_{\text{im}}$ is kept positive via projection during integration, $w(t) \ge -1$ holds by construction of that projection, not by an independent proof from metric positivity.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{SciPost Acceptance Criteria Fulfillment}
We address the acceptance criteria of \textit{SciPost Physics} as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Methodological Contribution}: It couples a high-performance Rust PDE solver with discrete algebraic consistency checks via Lean 4 in string theory. The solver itself is not formally verified; Lean validates discrete topological/algebraic invariants derived from its output, not the continuous physics.
    \item \textbf{Reproducibility Status}: All simulation scripts and Lean 4 sources are open-source and publicly archived. Locally, 83 theorems were audited against the project's axioms; 5 fail this audit (\texttt{buscher\_involution}, plus four theorems in the HoloAlg module, all resting on project-specific axioms), and the remaining 78 pass, though many are true by construction of their own definitions rather than by a derivation of the physics they name. Concerning \texttt{buscher\_involution} specifically: the axiom \texttt{inv\_inv} declared in \texttt{proofs/BuscherRules.lean}, instantiated at \texttt{Nat}, proves \texttt{False} in the Lean kernel, so this previously claimed result establishes nothing about Buscher T-duality and we withdraw that claim. Independently, the LeanMaster kernel-checked foundation (v2.2.0) verifies 188 theorems with 0 failing, depending only on Lean's three standard axioms; these are audit-gate counts on discrete algebraic statements, not simulation outputs. Performance/benchmark figures (throughput, timing, and cost comparisons) are pending hardware verification and are not reported in this letter or the revised manuscript.
    \item \textbf{Community Impact}: It introduces practical tools for Double Field Theory, generalized geometry, and Swampland cosmology, accompanied by a companion scientific software package (\texttt{leanflow}).
\end{itemize}
