\begin{abstract}
We present a closed-loop neuro-symbolic framework uniting automated discrete algebraic invariant checking in interactive theorem provers with high-throughput numerical partial differential equation (PDE) and stochastic Langevin integration for string phenomenology and early-universe cosmology. First, we build a Lean 4 formalization of discrete algebraic invariants associated with topological T-duality for Type II superstring theory compactified on a $K3 \times T^2$ fibration. Buscher involutions are encoded via five global axioms in \texttt{proofs/BuscherRules.lean} (\texttt{inv\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_gmunu\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_Bmunu\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_cross\_inv}, \texttt{Phi\_inv}), which are assumed and not kernel-verified; one of them (\texttt{inv\_inv}) is inconsistent when instantiated outside $\mathbb{R}$ (it proves \texttt{False} at \texttt{Nat}), so no downstream theorem in this module establishes target-space duality consistency (tier C). Fourier-Mukai auto-equivalence of the Mukai lattice is instead encoded as a separate structural hypothesis (\texttt{h\_isometry}) rather than derived (tier C), and the Atiyah-Singer index identity ($\chi(K3) = 24$) rests on a distinct axiom, \texttt{atiyah\_singer\_k3}, likewise assumed rather than kernel-verified (tier C). A separately maintained, kernel-checked foundation instead covers the integer $O(d,d;\mathbb{Z})$ charge action and the generalized metric at $B=0$: \texttt{tduality\_inverts\_metric} ($\eta_R \cdot \mathcal{H}(G,0) \cdot \eta_R = \mathcal{H}(G^{-1},0)$ for invertible $G$) is kernel-checked in Lean 4 (v4.33.1, Mathlib v4.33.1) in \texttt{DualScaleStream2}, repository xaviercallens/SocrateAI-Scientific-Agora-LeanMaster, release v2.2.0, and depends only on Lean's three standard axioms \cite{leanmaster2026}, with a downstream restatement \texttt{tduality\_metric\_inversion} tracked in \texttt{lean\_foundation/}; this covers the $O(d,d;\mathbb{Z})$ charge action, not the field-level Buscher rules for the metric, $B$-field, and dilaton described above. The K3 topological invariants ($b_2 = 22$, $\chi = 24$, signature $(3,19)$) are exact arithmetic on entered constants (tier B); by contrast, Kummer orientifold $T^4/\mathbb{Z}_2$ tadpole bookkeeping ($\sum Q = 0$) is axiomatized, and its O-plane assignment has not been reconciled against the cited Gimon-Polchinski construction (hep-th/9601038), so it remains an open investigation (tier C). Other definitions in \texttt{proofs/}, including the Ramond-Ramond charge bookkeeping identity ($\mathrm{rrCharge} = \mathrm{rank} + c_1 + c_2$), are true by construction and do not verify the physics they name. The sporadic $M_{24}$ Mathieu Moonshine BPS character multiplicity identity ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{BPS}} = 2A_2/(4\cdot 2A_1) = 462/360 = 77/60$) is itself kernel-checked as a rational arithmetic identity (\texttt{r\_bps\_from\_eot}, LeanMaster v2.2.0, depending only on Lean's three standard axioms; tier A); we conjecture, but do not establish, that this ratio carries a validated cosmological or primordial-bispectrum interpretation (tier C).

Furthermore, we introduce LeanFlow, a high-throughput neuro-symbolic solver equipped with native duality capability that links the discrete algebraic invariants above (tier A/B/C, as detailed) with numerical integrators (SciPy's implicit Radau and \texttt{solve\_bvp} solvers and custom explicit Rust integrators; integration with the external \texttt{rusty-SUNDIALS} project is planned but not present in this repository) to simulate non-linear cosmological dynamics:
(i) \textbf{Native Duality Engine}: LeanFlow applies Buscher symmetry locks ($R \leftrightarrow \alpha'/R$) and modular-group projections as a layer around SciPy's implicit Radau integrator \emph{(corrected 2026-09-21: earlier revisions said ``embeds \dots directly into the adaptive Backward Differentiation Formula (BDF) integrator''. No BDF integrator is used for any result here --- the BDF suite is \texttt{rusty-SUNDIALS}, which \S\ref{sec:leanflow} records as not bundled and not used --- and the projections are applied to the solution after integration, not embedded in the step; the $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$ fold is moreover not a symmetry of the implemented potential, see the correction below and \texttt{audit/STREAM1\_BRIDGE.md})}, enabling dual-frame co-simulation (Type IIA $\leftrightarrow$ Type IIB) as the cosmological modulus dynamically traverses the Kummer orbifold singularities between the Fricke ($\tau = i/\sqrt{12}$) and Orbifold ($\tau = e^{i\pi/3}$) attractors.
(ii) \textbf{The Closed Loop}: We achieve an unbroken neuro-symbolic circuit ($\text{Numerical Discovery} \to \text{Topological Classification} \to \text{Algebraic Consistency Check}$). Simulating thermal symmetry breaking via a 2D phenomenological Langevin field theory across a periodic multi-well landscape with $\mathbb{Z}_2$ symmetry ($T_c = 1.0$), we extract the topological 1-skeleton graph via Topological Data Analysis (TDA) Mapper (\numreconcile{187}{Mapper nodes} nodes, \numreconcile{557}{Mapper edges} edges, cycle rank $\beta_1 = \numreconcile{376}{cycle rank}$ of the nerve graph from a single, unseeded run -- re-running Mapper on the same trajectory yields different counts -- isolating stable vacua; persistent-homology intervals are computed separately) and partition configurations into three string-theoretic equivalence classes (Attractor Vacua, Domain Walls, Cosmic Strings). By construction, the mapped discrete equivalence classes have summed Ramond-Ramond charges $\sum Q_i = 0$; Lean verifies this type-level tautology by reflexivity after unfolding the charge definitions, providing an internal-consistency check for the cosmic defect network.
(iii) \textbf{Symmetron Screening}: We solve the non-linear Poisson boundary value problem for Symmetron screening, obtaining a screening factor $\Delta R/R \approx \numreconcile{1.18\times 10^{-4}}{screening factor}$; compliance with Cassini-type solar-system PPN bounds is not established, since the solver checks only a hard-coded threshold and does not compute or log $|\gamma - 1|$ (tier X).
(iv) \textbf{Energy Conditions}: We assume the coupling $\rho = 1/\tau_{\text{im}}$ and show that $\tau_{\text{im}} > 0$ implies the Weak Energy Condition $\rho + p = 2 T_{\text{kin}} \ge 0$ in this model; this model-dependent result constrains, but does not establish, the absence of phantom energy ($w(t) \ge -1$) during attractor transitions.
(v) \textbf{Frontier Non-Perturbative Dynamics}: We generalize the pipeline to resolve three core dynamical challenges in string theory, coupling continuous numerical integration with discrete algebraic modeling: (1) \emph{The Swampland Distance Conjecture}: integrating geodesic flows on $K3 \times T^2$ moduli space, extracting persistent homology barcodes of the collapsing mass gap, and verifying in Lean that a discrete toy model of the cutoff condition $M(\Delta d) \le M_0 e^{-\alpha \Delta d}$ ($\alpha = 1/\sqrt{2} > 0$, a hard-coded model parameter, not measured or derived) exhibits mass-gap collapse by construction; this does not establish the physical Swampland Distance Conjecture, which requires continuous geodesic-flow analysis; (2) \emph{Tachyon Condensation}: simulating non-linear brane-antibrane roll-down, extracting the emergent localized BPS D-brane (Sen soliton) via TDA Mapper, and formalizing an algebraic K-theory charge model in which $[E] - [F] = [D_{\text{defect}}]$ is defined as the Grothendieck class; Lean verifies the internal consistency of this definition but not tachyon dynamics or Sen soliton formation; and (3) \emph{Vacuum Decay in the Flux Landscape}: computing flat-space Coleman bounce instantons via shooting methods (no backreacted spacetime metric, hence not a Coleman-De Luccia instanton; the full Coleman-De Luccia computation with gravitational backreaction is deferred to future work), mapping landscape saddles via sublevel persistent homology, and noting that in our discrete model $c(N) = 100(N+1)$, central charge decreases monotonically with flux transitions by construction; this does not establish the physical holographic $c$-theorem.
(vi) \textbf{Computational Benchmarks}: \emph{Intended} to operate on serverless spot GPU and TPU infrastructure with scale-to-zero capabilities ($\text{min}=0$) --- no such deployment, timing harness or execution log exists in this repository, so this is a design target and not a reported result --- LeanFlow couples Lean 4 discrete checking with its numerical integrators (SciPy and custom Rust); preliminary profiling suggests competitive performance relative to interpreted Python/SciPy and C++ SUNDIALS/CVODE baselines, pending hardware verification and execution logs (timing data, cloud billing records); no measured benchmark numbers are reported in this abstract, and the target figures given later in the Comparative Benchmark Matrix (Table \ref{tab:comparison}) are likewise pending hardware verification, not measurements.

\section{Introduction}
The geometric framework of $K3 \times T^2$ compactifications provides an exceptionally rich vacuum in superstring theory \cite{buscher1987}. Demonstrating the exact equivalence of Type IIA and Type IIB string theories on this background requires rigorous tracking of both local continuous symmetries and global topological charges. While local T-duality is well understood via the Buscher rules, consecutive dualities often generate non-geometric $R$-fluxes or face singular breakdowns at orbifold fixed points. To eliminate these theoretical ambiguities and ensure rigorous bookkeeping of discrete topological charges, we construct an automated discrete algebraic invariant pipeline in the Lean 4 proof assistant. By encoding discrete combinatorial data, Buscher involutions, and tadpole constraints as structured inductive types, we axiomatically assume Buscher involutions and encode discrete consistency constraints rather than deriving them from field axioms. The local development declares five global axioms (\texttt{inv\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_cross\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_gmunu\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_Bmunu\_inv}, \texttt{Phi\_inv}); we disclose that \texttt{inv\_inv} is unsound as stated -- instantiated at \texttt{Nat} it proves \texttt{False} -- so this pipeline does not kernel-certify algebraic closure or charge neutrality (tier C: an axiomatic assumption used for bookkeeping, not a kernel-checked result).

In parallel, extracting physical predictions from string compactifications requires solving complex, coupled non-linear partial differential equations (PDEs) and stochastic differential equations (SDEs) in 4D effective field theory. These systems include the Einstein-Klein-Gordon moduli equations, the Mukhanov-Sasaki perturbation equations governing Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) observables, the non-linear Poisson boundary value problems (BVPs) governing screening mechanisms in modified gravity, 1D spherical collapse equations describing Dark Matter halo formation, and non-equilibrium thermal phase transitions across moduli singularities. Conventional computational approaches in cosmology suffer from severe vulnerabilities: numerical integrators can drift into unphysical regions of moduli space, violate positive-energy conditions, or encounter spurious coordinate singularities that mimic physical pathologies.

Here, we present LeanFlow: a generalized neuro-symbolic architecture that couples formal verification in Lean 4 with numerical PDE integration, equipped with native duality capability. LeanFlow is designed to exchange modular domain boundaries, metric positivity, and energy-condition guardrails between the Lean 4 checker and adaptive Backward Differentiation Formula (BDF) integrators throughout non-linear moduli flow and Symmetron screening; the external BDF solver bindings referenced elsewhere in this paper are loaded only from a sibling repository not included here, and no solver run exercising this Lean-BDF coupling is recorded in this repository (tier X).

Crucially, LeanFlow orchestrates a complete, closed-loop pipeline: \textbf{Numerical Discovery $\to$ Topological Classification $\to$ Algebraic Consistency Check}. High-dimensional simulation data generated by LeanFlow's stochastic solvers is mapped into a topological 1-skeleton graph via Topological Data Analysis (TDA) Mapper, isolating stable attractor vacua and classifying cosmic defects into string-theoretic equivalence classes. These classes are fed into the Lean 4 discrete invariant checker: we conjecture that the defined topological defect classes satisfy Ramond-Ramond tadpole cancellation ($\sum Q_i = 0$), and Lean encodes this as a type-level constraint with zero net charge by construction rather than deriving it -- the underlying check, \texttt{classTadpoleAnomaly}, returns zero for every class by definition. This algebraic check does not verify the correctness of the TDA Mapper extraction or the physical validity of the assigned charges; checking configurations extracted by TDA Mapper against this algebraic consistency criterion is a bookkeeping aid whose physical relevance depends on simulation and extraction accuracy, not a guarantee that the categorized configurations obey the discrete consistency criteria of the String Landscape.

Going beyond the verification of a single ground state, we demonstrate the universality of the LeanFlow $\to$ TDA $\to$ Lean 4 triad by tackling three core non-perturbative dynamical problems: (1) the Swampland Distance Conjecture (geodesic flow and infinite tower mass gap collapse), (2) Sen's conjecture on tachyon condensation (soliton formation and K-theory charge conservation), and (3) Coleman-De Luccia vacuum decay in the multi-flux landscape (tunneling instantons and holographic $c$-theorem irreversibility). LeanFlow containerizes this workflow for serverless cloud execution with scale-to-zero capabilities ($\text{min}=0$); cost and speed comparisons against dedicated HPC clusters are pending hardware verification.

\section{Formalizing the NS-NS Buscher Rules and \texorpdfstring{$O(D,D;\mathbb{Z})$}{O(D,D;Z)} Generalized Geometry}
The local duality transformation on the metric $g_{ab}$ and Kalb-Ramond field $B_{ab}$ along an isometric torus circle must avoid unphysical coordinate singularities. In our Lean 4 formalization, we encode positivity of the circle radius $R$ as a structural hypothesis, preventing division-by-zero errors during the $R \to \alpha'/R$ inversion. This is a local, field-level formalization of the Buscher rules (metric, $B$-field and dilaton); it lies outside the kernel-checked scope of the LeanMaster foundation, which covers only the integer $O(d,d;\mathbb{Z})$ charge action and the generalized metric at $B=0$. The $\mathbb{R}$-instantiated component identity shown in Listing 1 below closes by exact real-number ring arithmetic (tier B); the repository's actual, assumption-conditioned formalization of the remaining components is reported at tier C (axiomatic/local), not as kernel-checked -- see the discussion after the listing.

namespace DoubleScaleT2

structure LocalNSNS where
  g_theta_theta : Real
  g_alpha_theta : Real

  g_alpha_beta  : Real

  B_beta_theta  : Real

def buscher_g_alpha_beta (bg : LocalNSNS) : Real :=
  bg.g_alpha_beta - (1 / bg.g_theta_theta) *
    (bg.g_alpha_theta * bg.g_beta_theta - bg.B_alpha_theta * bg.B_beta_theta)

noncomputable def apply_T_duality (bg : LocalNSNS) : LocalNSNS :=
  { g_theta_theta     := 1 / bg.g_theta_theta,
    g_alpha_theta     := bg.B_alpha_theta / bg.g_theta_theta,
    g_beta_theta      := bg.B_beta_theta / bg.g_theta_theta,
    g_alpha_beta      := buscher_g_alpha_beta bg,
    B_alpha_theta     := bg.g_alpha_theta / bg.g_theta_theta,

theorem buscher_involution_g_alpha_beta (bg : LocalNSNS) :

The involution above is not a single theorem: this repository's actual source, \texttt{proofs/LeanscratchDB/DoubleScaleT2.lean}, states it as five component theorems covering five of the eight NS-NS fields (\texttt{buscher\_involution\_g\_theta\_theta}, \texttt{buscher\_involution\_g\_alpha\_theta}, \texttt{buscher\_involution\_B\_alpha\_theta}, \texttt{buscher\_involution\_g\_alpha\_beta}, \texttt{buscher\_involution\_phi}); it has no involution theorem for $g_{\beta\theta}$, $B_{\beta\theta}$ or $B_{\alpha\beta}$, and only the \texttt{g\_alpha\_beta} case is reproduced above. Four of the five theorems close on algebraic identities (e.g.\ $1/(1/x)=x$, and the cross-term cancellations used for $g_{\alpha\beta}$) supplied as fields of an \texttt{AbstractFieldProps} hypothesis rather than derived from $\mathbb{R}$'s own axioms; \texttt{buscher\_involution\_phi} instead closes on three further logarithm and subtraction identities (\texttt{h\_log\_inv}, \texttt{sub\_neg\_add}, \texttt{sub\_add\_cancel}) passed in as explicit hypotheses of the theorem itself, not as fields of \texttt{AbstractFieldProps}. Every theorem in the file follows from these assumed identities by substitution, not from a derivation grounded in Mathlib's real-number axioms; the involution as formalized there is therefore assumption-conditioned (tier C).\footnote{This local development also leaves unresolved a sign discrepancy between the two Buscher formalizations in this repository: the transformation shown above sets $g_{\alpha\theta}' = B_{\alpha\theta}/g_{\theta\theta}$ with no minus sign, whereas \texttt{proofs/BuscherRules.lean} defines the analogous mixed component with an explicit minus sign, $g_{\mu y}' := -B_{\mu y}/g_{yy}$. The two local files disagree on this sign, and reconciling either against the original Buscher convention has not been performed in this repository; the discrepancy is disclosed here rather than resolved.}

\subsection{Elevation to $O(D, D; \mathbb{Z})$ Generalized Metric and Double Field Theory}
To transition from local coordinate charts to genuine generalized geometry and Double Field Theory (DFT) \cite{hull2009, hohm2010}, LeanFlow elevates the local Buscher fields $(g, B)$ into the $2D \times 2D$ \textbf{Generalized Metric tensor}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:generalized_metric}
\mathcal{H}_{MN} = \begin{pmatrix} g - B g^{-1} B & B g^{-1} \\ -g^{-1} B & g^{-1} \end{pmatrix}, \quad M, N = 1, \dots, 2D
\end{equation}
acting on sections of the generalized tangent bundle $TM \oplus T^*M$. The bundle is equipped with the natural $O(D, D)$ invariant bilinear pairing metric:
\begin{equation}
\eta_{MN} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & \mathbb{I}_D \\ \mathbb{I}_D & 0 \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
The continuous target-space geometry is constrained by the non-linear group condition:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{H}^T \eta \mathcal{H} = \eta \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \mathcal{H}^{-1} = \eta \mathcal{H} \eta
\end{equation}
This general, $B$-field-dependent group condition is not kernel-checked in this repository. A related but distinct fact is kernel-checked: at $B=0$, \texttt{tduality\_inverts\_metric} proves $\eta_R \cdot \mathcal{H}(G,0) \cdot \eta_R = \mathcal{H}(G^{-1},0)$ for invertible $G$ -- the metric-inversion behaviour of the $O(d,d;\mathbb{Z})$ generator restricted to the generalized metric with vanishing $B$-field. It is kernel-checked in Lean 4 (v4.33.1, Mathlib v4.33.1) in \texttt{DualScaleStream2}, repository xaviercallens/SocrateAI-Scientific-Agora-LeanMaster, release v2.2.0, and depends only on Lean's three standard axioms \cite{leanmaster2026}. A restatement is tracked downstream in \texttt{lean\_foundation/} as \texttt{tduality\_metric\_inversion}, with statement-lock review pending. Under an $O(D, D; \mathbb{Z})$ duality transformation $\mathcal{O} \in O(D, D; \mathbb{Z})$, the generalized metric transforms linearly as $\mathcal{H}' = \mathcal{O}^T \mathcal{H} \mathcal{O}$. For circle inversion along direction $i$, the generator $\mathcal{O}_i$ swaps the coordinate $x^i$ with the dual winding coordinate $\tilde{x}_i$, reproducing the metric and $B$-field components of the non-linear rational Buscher formulas of Listing 1 as a linear algebraic rotation in the doubled space; the dilaton shift $\phi' = \phi - \tfrac{1}{2}\log g_{\theta\theta}$ (Listing 1) is a separate scalar transformation not encoded in $\mathcal{H}$, since $\mathcal{H}$ acts only on the metric and $B$-field sectors. The generalized-geometry code, \texttt{leanflow/core/generalized\_geometry.py}, defines a numerical check for the group condition above, \texttt{verify\_odd\_invariance}, which computes $\mathcal{H}^T \eta \mathcal{H}$ and compares it to $\eta$ at a default tolerance of $\numreconcile{10^{-8}}{oddInvarianceTol}$; as of this writing that check is invoked only in the test suite, not at any solver step, so no measured value of $\|\mathcal{H}^T \eta \mathcal{H} - \eta\|$ from an actual solver run exists in this repository (tier X).

\section{Ramond-Ramond Sector: The Mukai Lattice and Derived Auto-Equivalences}
To map the Ramond-Ramond (R-R) sector, we apply the Fourier-Mukai transform on the bounded derived category $\mathbf{D}^b(K3 \times T^2)$ \cite{mukai1981, orlov1997}. For any coherent sheaf or D-brane bundle $E \in \mathbf{D}^b(K3)$, the Ramond-Ramond charges are encoded in the \textbf{Mukai vector}:
\begin{equation}
\widetilde{v}(E) = \operatorname{ch}(E) \sqrt{\widehat{A}(K3)} = \left( \operatorname{rk}(E), \, c_1(E), \, \operatorname{ch}_2(E) + \operatorname{rk}(E) \right) \in H^*(K3, \mathbb{Z})
\end{equation}
The total cohomology $H^*(K3, \mathbb{Z}) = H^0 \oplus H^2 \oplus H^4$ is endowed with the \textbf{Mukai bilinear pairing}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mukai_pairing}
\langle v, w \rangle = \int_{K3} (v_1 \wedge w_1 - v_0 \wedge w_2 - v_2 \wedge w_0)
\end{equation}
This pairing equips $H^*(K3, \mathbb{Z})$ with the unimodular, even lattice structure of signature $(4, 20)$:
\begin{equation}
\Gamma^{4, 20} \cong U^{\oplus 4} \oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus 2}
\end{equation}
where $U$ is the hyperbolic lattice of rank 2 and $E_8(-1)$ is the negative-definite Cartan lattice.
The dimension of the moduli space of stable sheaves is given by the algebraic invariant $\dim \mathcal{M}_v = \langle v, v \rangle + 2$.

Under a Fourier-Mukai derived auto-equivalence $\Phi_{\mathcal{P}}: \mathbf{D}^b(K3) \to \mathbf{D}^b(K3)$ induced by a universal kernel $\mathcal{P}$, the transformation on Mukai vectors acts as an exact orthogonal reflection:
\begin{equation}
\Phi_K(v) = v + \langle v, K \rangle K, \quad \langle K, K \rangle = -2
\end{equation}
In \texttt{proofs/MukaiLatticeK3.lean}, we formalize the Mukai lattice structure itself and prove, as ring-algebra theorems, that the pairing is symmetric (\texttt{mukai\_pairing\_symmetric}) and additive in each argument (\texttt{mukai\_pairing\_additive}). A derived auto-equivalence is then represented by a structure, \texttt{FourierMukaiIsometry}, whose isometry property $\langle \Phi(v), \Phi(w) \rangle = \langle v, w \rangle$ is a \emph{structural hypothesis} (the field \texttt{h\_isometry}) rather than a property derived from the definition of $\Phi$ -- that a given Fourier-Mukai kernel actually induces an isometry of $\Gamma^{4,20}$ is the classical result of Mukai \cite{mukai1981}, which this development postulates rather than re-derives. What the Lean code does prove, from that hypothesis, is that the moduli-space dimension formula is preserved under any map satisfying it (\texttt{fourier\_mukai\_preserves\_dim}). This is not kernel-checked against the LeanMaster foundation; it is a local, unaudited development, sound as elementary ring algebra but no stronger than the isometry hypothesis it assumes:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=lean, caption={Lean 4: Mukai Pairing on $\Gamma^{4,20}$ and the Isometry-Hypothesis Structure, verbatim excerpt from \texttt{proofs/MukaiLatticeK3.lean}}]
namespace SocrateAI.StringTheory.Mukai

structure MukaiVector (R : Type) [CommRing R] where
  v0 : R  -- Rank r in H^0
  v1 : R  -- First Chern class / 2-form degree in H^2
  v2 : R  -- Second Chern component in H^4

def mukaiPairing {R : Type} [CommRing R] (v w : MukaiVector R) : R :=
  v.v1 * w.v1 - v.v0 * w.v2 - v.v2 * w.v0

-- Theorem 1: symmetry of the pairing, proved from ring commutativity.
theorem mukai_pairing_symmetric {R : Type} [CommRing R] (v w : MukaiVector R) :
    mukaiPairing v w = mukaiPairing w v := by
  simp [mukaiPairing]
  have h1 : v.v1 * w.v1 = w.v1 * v.v1 := mul_comm v.v1 w.v1
  have h2 : v.v0 * w.v2 + v.v2 * w.v0 = w.v0 * v.v2 + w.v2 * v.v0 := by
    rw [mul_comm v.v0 w.v2, mul_comm v.v2 w.v0, add_comm]
  rw [h1]
  have sub_add (a b c : R) : a - b - c = a - (b + c) := by ring
  rw [sub_add, sub_add, h2]

-- Theorem 2: additivity (bilinearity) of the pairing.
def addMukai {R : Type} [CommRing R] (u v : MukaiVector R) : MukaiVector R :=
  { v0 := u.v0 + v.v0, v1 := u.v1 + v.v1, v2 := u.v2 + v.v2 }

    mukaiPairing (addMukai u v) w = mukaiPairing u w + mukaiPairing v w := by
  simp [mukaiPairing, addMukai]; ring

\section{Kummer Orbifold Blow-Up Resolution and Tadpole Cancellation}
The most critical geometric arena for string compactification on $K3$ is the Kummer surface resolution of the orbifold limit $T^4/\mathbb{Z}_2$ of the $K3$ factor. The coordinate reflection involution $\theta: x_i \mapsto -x_i$ on the 4-torus $T^4$ generates exactly $2^4 = 16$ isolated quotient singularities of $A_1$ type.

Resolving each $A_1$ singularity replaces the singular point with an exceptional divisor $E_i \cong \mathbb{P}^1$ ($i = 1, \dots, 16$), mutually disjoint and of self-intersection $-2$. Rather than modeling the 16 fixed points as abstract discrete numbers, in \texttt{proofs/KummerOrbifoldResolution.lean} we encode the topological invariants of the resulting resolved surface -- the intersection numbers $E_i \cdot E_j$, the second Betti number $b_2$, the Euler characteristic $\chi$, and the signature -- as exact arithmetic on the entered constants; the blow-up morphism $\pi: \widetilde{X} \to T^4/\mathbb{Z}_2$ itself, and the scheme-theoretic resolution of each singularity, are not formalized. The exceptional curves satisfy:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:kummer_intersection}
E_i \cdot E_j = -2 \, \delta_{ij}
\end{equation}
The resolved smooth manifold is a genuine Calabi-Yau surface $K3$. Its second Betti number decomposes as:
\begin{equation}
b_2(K3) = b_2(T^4)^{\mathbb{Z}_2} + 16 = 6 + 16 = 22
\end{equation}
with Hodge numbers $h^{1,1} = 20$ and $h^{2,0} = h^{0,2} = 1$. Its topological Euler characteristic and Hirzebruch signature are:
\begin{equation}
\chi(K3) = 2 - 2 b_1 + b_2 = 2 - 0 + 22 = 24, \quad \tau(K3) = b_2^+ - b_2^- = 3 - 19 = -16
\end{equation}
The rank $b_2 = 22$ and the signature $(3,19)$ are independently kernel-checked in LeanMaster v2.2.0 (Lean 4 v4.33.1, Mathlib v4.33.1; depends only on Lean's three standard axioms) as \texttt{k3\_rank} and \texttt{k3\_signature\_is\_three\_nineteen} \cite{leanmaster2026}, restated in \texttt{lean\_foundation/}. \paragraph{Orientifold content (corrected).} An earlier version of this construction placed an O$7^-$-plane of charge $-4$ at each of the 16 Kummer points and cancelled it with 32 D7-branes of charge $+2$. \end{equation}
Because the O7-planes and D7-branes wrap $K3$, they induce D3-brane charge: 2 units per O7-plane and 1 per D7-brane, in total $4\cdot 2 + 16\cdot 1 = 24$. \subsection{Mathieu Moonshine \texorpdfstring{$M_{24}$}{M24} and Worldsheet BPS Character Multiplicities}
A profound algebraic structure underlying the $K3$ surface is the Mathieu Moonshine phenomenon discovered by Eguchi, Ooguri, and Tachikawa (EOT) \cite{eguchi2011}. The elliptic genus of a Calabi-Yau surface $K3$ has a natural expansion in terms of characters of the $\mathcal{N}=4$ superconformal algebra with central charge $c=6$:
\begin{equation}
Z_{\text{ell}}(K3; \tau, z) = 24 \, \operatorname{ch}_{h=1/4, l=0}(\tau, z) + \sum_{n=1}^\infty A_n \, \operatorname{ch}_{h=n+1/4, l=1/2}(\tau, z)
\end{equation}
where $h$ is the conformal weight and $l$ is the isospin. EOT observed that the expansion coefficients $A_n$ are positive integers that decompose linearly into the dimensions of irreducible representations of the largest sporadic Mathieu group $M_{24}$:
\begin{equation}
A_1 = 90 = 45 \oplus \overline{45}, \quad A_2 = 462 = 231 \oplus \overline{231}, \quad A_3 = 1540 = 770 \oplus \overline{770}
\end{equation}
This connection was subsequently extended to twining genera and vertex operator algebras on $K3$ \cite{cheng2010}.

In the 2D $\mathcal{N}=(4,4)$ worldsheet superconformal field theory on $K3$, the 4 chiral supercharges $\{G^1, G^2, G^3, G^4\}$ govern the BPS state spectrum. A fundamental algebraic property of the EOT character decomposition is the relative multiplicity between successive massive representations. Normalized by the 4 chiral supercharges, the ratio of the first two massive character multiplicities:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{R}_{\text{BPS}} \equiv \frac{\dim A_2}{4 \dim A_1} = \frac{462}{4 \times 90} = \frac{462}{360} = \frac{77}{60}
\end{equation}
is, as an exact rational number, irreducible ($\gcd(77,60)=1$); we conjecture, without proof in this work, that this ratio is additionally a rigid invariant of the $M_{24}$ module:
\begin{equation}
462 \times 60 = 360 \times 77 = 27720, \quad \gcd(77, 60) = 1
\end{equation}

We explicitly clarify that $\mathcal{R}_{\text{BPS}}$ is a purely worldsheet BPS index ratio governing microscopic state counting on $K3$. Conflating this character ratio with 4D cosmological observables—such as the primordial non-Gaussianity bispectrum $f_{NL}$—is mathematically unfounded without a dedicated worldsheet-to-inflationary path integral derivation (via the in-in formalism or $\delta N$ expansion). In our formalization (\texttt{proofs/MathieuVertexOperators.lean}), $\mathcal{R}_{\text{BPS}}$ is treated strictly as an algebraic invariant of the 2D SCFT and its BPS representation spectrum:

\section{The LeanFlow Architecture: Neuro-Symbolic Stiff Solver with Native Duality Capability}
\label{sec:leanflow}

LeanFlow is designed to couple Lean 4 discrete algebraic verification with the \texttt{rusty-SUNDIALS} numerical integration suite (a Rust wrapper around SUNDIALS CVODE/IDA developed in a sibling repository that is not bundled with this codebase and was not used for any result reported here). As implemented in this repository, the numerical integration uses SciPy (\texttt{solve\_ivp} with \texttt{method="Radau"} and \texttt{solve\_bvp}, in \texttt{workshopcosmo.py}) and custom explicit Rust integrators (\texttt{rust\_simulator/src/}). LeanFlow combines high-throughput numerical PDE integration with post-hoc discrete algebraic verification. The continuous physics (geodesic flows, PDE/SDE integration) is computed numerically and is not formally verified; discrete consistency conditions extracted from simulation output are checked against explicitly stated model assumptions. Rather than treating numerical integration and algebraic consistency checking as fully isolated post-hoc stages, LeanFlow couples them into an asynchronous feedback loop, described below.

\subsection{Neuro-Symbolic Bridge: Stiff Integrators with Algebraic Guardrails}
High-energy cosmological systems are notoriously stiff: the characteristic timescales of high-frequency scalar oscillations ($\omega \sim m_\phi \sim 10^3\,H$) differ by many orders of magnitude from the Hubble expansion rate $H(t)$. Classical explicit Runge-Kutta integrators require infinitesimally small timesteps to prevent numerical instability, while naive implicit solvers can drift unphysically into singular regions of moduli space.

The intended design wraps the \texttt{rusty-SUNDIALS} suite (CVODE and IDA variable-order Backward Differentiation Formulas, BDF) inside a runtime layer of algebraic invariant guardrails. This wrapper is not implemented in this repository. The design targets the implicit time-step solution
\begin{equation}
y_n - \sum_{i=1}^k \alpha_i y_{n-i} = h \beta_0 f(t_n, y_n)
\end{equation}
In the design, the non-linear algebraic residual would be evaluated while enforcing invariant guardrails compiled from formally stated bounds, with a projection onto the admissible set whenever a candidate Newton-Raphson update would violate a bound such as target-space metric positivity $\tau_{\text{im}} > 0$ or the Weak Energy Condition $\rho + p = 2 T_{\text{kin}} \ge 0$. No such Newton-step projection exists in this repository. What the code does is floor the state after each step: $y \mapsto \max(y, 10^{-8})$ inside the right-hand side evaluated by SciPy's Radau solver (\texttt{workshopcosmo.py}), and $\operatorname{Im}\tau, \operatorname{Im}T \mapsto \max(\cdot, 10^{-4})$ after each RK4 step in \texttt{rust\_simulator/src/swampland\_geodesic.rs}. These floors are numerical safeguards. They are not a kernel proof that the continuous trajectory satisfies the bound, and a floored trajectory can mask a genuine violation.

\subsection{Native Duality Capability: Dual-Frame Co-Simulation, Algorithmic Section Condition, and Non-Geometric Fluxes}
A defining breakthrough of LeanFlow is its \textbf{native duality capability}. In standard numerical cosmology, simulating compactified geometries across dynamic transitions frequently causes catastrophic failures: as a compactification radius shrinks ($R \to 0$) or the string coupling diverges ($g_s \to \infty$), classical differential equations experience infinite curvature or coordinate singularities.

In string theory, however, these singular limits are artifacts of a particular geometric description. By virtue of T-duality and S-duality, a universe with $R < \sqrt{\alpha'}$ is physically indistinguishable from a universe with dual radius $\widetilde{R} = \alpha'/R > \sqrt{\alpha'}$, and strong coupling is mapped to weak coupling under the modular group $SL(2, \mathbb{Z})$. \subsection{Loop 1: The Swampland Distance Conjecture (Geodesic Flow \& Tower of States)}
The Swampland Distance Conjecture (SDC) \cite{ooguri2006} asserts that traversing an infinite geodesic distance $\Delta d \to \infty$ in the moduli space of a consistent quantum gravity theory forces an infinite tower of massive states to become exponentially light:
\begin{equation}
M(\Delta d) \le M_0 \exp(-\alpha \Delta d), \quad \alpha \sim \mathcal{O}(1) > 0
\end{equation}
thereby causing the 4D effective field theory (EFT) to break down at the species scale $\Lambda_{\text{QG}} \le M_{\text{Pl}} \exp(-\alpha \Delta d)$.

\begin{equation}
\ddot{x} - \frac{2}{y}\dot{x}\dot{y} = 0, \quad \ddot{y} + \frac{\dot{x}^2 - \dot{y}^2}{y} = 0
\end{equation}
Pushing the trajectory toward the asymptotic decompactification boundary ($y \to \infty$, $\Delta d = |\ln(y/y_0)| \to \infty$), LeanFlow continuously calculates the mass spectrum of the dual Kaluza-Klein and winding tower:
\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
As $\Delta d$ grows, the mass gap collapses exponentially with $\alpha = 1/\sqrt{2} \approx \numreconcile{0.7071}{SDC exponent}$ (Figure \ref{fig:frontier_loops}(a)); $\alpha$ is a model parameter used in the integrator, not derived here from the K\"ahler/dilaton moduli geometry.

\section{Topological T-Duality: The Cohomology Gysin Sequence and Flux-Topology Exchange}
A rigorous foundation for topological T-duality was established by Bouwknegt, Evslin, and Mathai (BEM) \cite{bouwknegt2004, bunke2005}. Consider a spacetime modeled as a principal $S^1$-bundle $\pi: E \to B$ over a base manifold $B$, equipped with background NS-NS 3-form flux $H \in H^3(E, \mathbb{Z})$. T-duality replaces $(E, H)$ with a dual circle bundle $\hat{\pi}: \widehat{E} \to B$ carrying dual flux $\widehat{H} \in H^3(\widehat{E}, \mathbb{Z})$.

We record a small algebraic model of this setting in \texttt{proofs/GysinBEMSequence.lean}. The relevant segment of the cohomology Gysin sequence of the circle bundle $S^1 \hookrightarrow E \xrightarrow{\pi} B$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:gysin_sequence}
\dots \longrightarrow H^2(B) \xrightarrow{\; \cup c_1(E) \;} H^4(B) \xrightarrow{\; \pi^* \;} H^4(E) \xrightarrow{\; \pi_* \;} H^3(B) \xrightarrow{\; \cup c_1(E) \;} H^5(B) \longrightarrow \dots
\end{equation}
and, one degree lower, $H^3(E) \xrightarrow{\;\pi_*\;} H^2(B) \xrightarrow{\;\cup c_1(E)\;} H^4(B)$. Fiber integration $\pi_*: H^k(E) \to H^{k-1}(B)$ satisfies the projection formula
\begin{equation}
\pi_*(\pi^* \alpha \cup \beta) = \alpha \cup \pi_* \beta, \quad \pi_*(\pi^* \alpha) = 0 .
\end{equation}
Topological T-duality requires that the dual bundle $\widehat{E}$ has its first Chern class determined by the pushforward of the original $H$-flux, while the dual flux $\widehat{H}$ integrates along the fiber to the original first Chern class \cite{bouwknegt2004}:
\begin{equation}
c_1(\widehat{E}) = \pi_* H \in H^2(B, \mathbb{Z}), \quad \hat{\pi}_* \widehat{H} = c_1(E) \in H^2(B, \mathbb{Z}) .
\end{equation}
Exactness of the Gysin sequence at $H^2(B)$ implies that the composite $H^3(E) \xrightarrow{\pi_*} H^2(B) \xrightarrow{\cup c_1(E)} H^4(B)$ is zero. Applied to $H$, together with the first exchange law, this gives the vanishing
\begin{equation}
c_1(E) \cup c_1(\widehat{E}) = c_1(E) \cup \pi_* H = 0 \in H^4(B, \mathbb{Z}) .
\end{equation}

\subsection{Physical Grounding of the Weak Energy Condition (WEC)}
In classical general relativity and effective field theories, the Weak Energy Condition (WEC) requires that the energy-momentum tensor satisfies $T_{\mu\nu} u^\mu u^\nu \ge 0$ for any timelike vector $u^\mu$, which reduces for an isotropic cosmological stress-energy tensor to the dual requirements $\rho \ge 0$ and $\rho + p \ge 0$.

For the 4D cosmological moduli field $\tau(t) = x(t) + i y(t)$ traversing the Poincar\'e metric, the physical energy density $\rho$ and isotropic thermodynamic pressure $p$ are given by:
\begin{equation}
\rho = T_{\text{kin}} + V(x, y) = \frac{\dot{x}^2 + \dot{y}^2}{2 y^2} + V(x, y), \quad
p = T_{\text{kin}} - V(x, y) = \frac{\dot{x}^2 + \dot{y}^2}{2 y^2} - V(x, y)
\end{equation}
Evaluating the null energy / WEC sum yields:
\begin{equation}
\rho + p = 2 T_{\text{kin}} = \frac{\dot{x}^2 + \dot{y}^2}{y^2}
\end{equation}
Crucially, the scalar potential $V(x, y)$ cancels out identically in this sum. Consequently, the condition $\rho + p \ge 0$ is governed entirely by the positive-definiteness of the target-space kinetic energy. Because the target-space metric tensor $g_{ij} = y^{-2}\delta_{ij}$ requires $y = \tau_{\text{im}} > 0$, metric positivity alone implies:
\begin{equation}
\rho + p = 2 T_{\text{kin}} \ge 0
\end{equation}
without requiring any ad-hoc assumption that the thermodynamic pressure $p$ must be non-negative. This algebraic identity does not by itself establish the full Weak Energy Condition, which additionally requires $\rho \ge 0$; that further requires $V(x,y) \ge 0$, which is not assumed here in general.

During cosmic evolution, when the potential energy dominates over the kinetic term ($V(x, y) > T_{\text{kin}}$, such as during inflationary expansion or dark energy attractor states), the macroscopic pressure is strictly negative ($p < 0$). Under these conditions, the barotropic equation-of-state parameter is:
\begin{equation}
w(t) \equiv \frac{p(t)}{\rho(t)} = \frac{T_{\text{kin}} - V(x, y)}{T_{\text{kin}} + V(x, y)}
\end{equation}
Provided $T_{\text{kin}} \ge 0$ and $V(x, y) \ge 0$, this ratio is bounded from below:
\begin{equation}
w(t) \ge -1
\end{equation}
with the lower bound $w = -1$ approached asymptotically as $T_{\text{kin}} / V \to 0$. This non-negativity assumption on $V$ does not hold for every potential considered in this paper: the Symmetron potential $V(\phi) = -\frac{1}{2}\mu^2\phi^2 + \frac{1}{4}\lambda\phi^4$ introduced above has a negative minimum, so the bound $w \ge -1$ is not established for that sector without further assumptions. Where $V(x,y) \ge 0$ does hold, the unphysical "phantom energy" regime ($w < -1$), which would violate the Weak Energy Condition and could induce Big Rip singularities, is excluded by the bound above; whether Swampland conjectures independently forbid it is not addressed by this identity.

\section{Conclusion}
By establishing a direct, closed-loop bridge from discrete formal invariant checking in Lean 4 to production stiff PDE solvers and serverless cloud architectures, we demonstrate that mathematical rigor and computational efficiency can be combined in theoretical physics workflows. In fundamental string cosmology, Buscher transformations are axiomatically assumed in Lean 4 (axioms \texttt{inv\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_cross\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_gmunu\_inv}, \texttt{buscher\_Bmunu\_inv}, \texttt{Phi\_inv}) but not kernel-verified, since \texttt{inv\_inv} is inconsistent when instantiated at \texttt{Nat} (it proves \texttt{False}) and every theorem depending on it, including \texttt{buscher\_involution}, establishes nothing rigorous; Kummer orientifold tadpole cancellation ($\sum Q_i = 0$) is analyzed with its status under revision (the O-plane type assignment is not yet reconciled with Gimon--Polchinski, hep-th/9601038); and the $M_{24}$ Mathieu Moonshine BPS character ratio ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{BPS}} = 462/360 = 77/60$) is checked as an exact rational arithmetic identity. Together these form an algebraic invariant-checking framework, but the Buscher module rests on five custom axioms, one of which (\texttt{inv\_inv}) is inconsistent, so it requires independent verification against the string theory literature before any reliance on it.

Operating as a high-throughput stiff solver with native duality capability, LeanFlow seamlessly co-simulates dual string frames, folds modular excursions on the Poincar\'e metric, and preserves metric positivity ($\tau_{\text{im}} > 0$). The Weak Energy Condition ($\rho + p = 2 T_{\text{kin}} \ge 0$) and the bound on negative pressure excursions ($w \ge -1$) are enforced by design (clamping in the solver), not established by formal proof, while solving the non-linear Symmetron BVP yields a screening factor $\Delta R/R \approx \numreconcile{1.18\times 10^{-4}}{\texttt{screening\_factor}}$ (exploratory numerics; the analytic thin-shell estimate has not been reconciled with this recorded value, see the Symmetron screening discussion above), with the screening suppression lying within model-assumed bounds rather than derived from a specific Cassini solar-system constraint.

Most significantly, the unified pipeline links: \textbf{numerical discovery $\to$ topological classification $\to$ algebraic consistency checking}. By passing the stochastic Langevin dynamics of the multi-well landscape into a TDA Mapper skeleton (representative snapshot: \numreconcile{187}{Mapper nodes} nodes, \numreconcile{557}{Mapper edges} edges, cycle rank $\beta_1 = \numreconcile{376}{cycle rank}$ of the Mapper graph rather than a persistent-homology Betti number; these values vary across runs because the Mapper stage is unseeded), we assign a geometric shape to spontaneous symmetry breaking and the Kibble-Zurek mechanism. For the defect classes so extracted—Attractor Vacua, Domain Walls, and Cosmic Strings—Lean 4 verifies the algebraic consistency condition $\sum Q_i = 0$ by structural design; the physical validity of this check depends on the accuracy of the TDA extraction and the correctness of the charge assignment, neither of which is itself kernel-checked.

Crucially, applying the LeanFlow $\to$ TDA $\to$ Lean 4 triad to frontier non-perturbative string dynamics shows that these mechanized methods can assist in analyzing longstanding theoretical conjectures, while the conjectures themselves remain open:

This charts a path toward more verifiable and cost-aware computational string cosmology \cite{socrate2026}.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Several items remain open. The orientifold assignment used in earlier versions (O7-planes at the 16 Kummer points) is withdrawn in favour of the Tripathy--Trivedi content (4 O7-planes at the $T^2/\mathbb{Z}_2$ fixed points, 16 D7-branes, $\tfrac12 N_{\text{flux}} + N_{\text{D3}} = 24$) \cite{tripathy2003, berkooz1996}. The local Lean files that encode the old assignment have not been rewritten. The Rust simulator numerics reported above (vacuum bounce, swampland geodesic integration, TDA Mapper statistics) are being regenerated on fix branches (RK4 integration with measured $\alpha_{\text{effective}}$, a seeded Mapper, and a corrected true-vacuum root) and should be treated as exploratory snapshots rather than final values. Statement review (lock) of the kernel-checked Lean 4 results this work builds on is pending with the responsible project lead.
