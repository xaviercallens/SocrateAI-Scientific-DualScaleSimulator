# Reverse-to-zero, round E3/E4: where the theory stands and what would actually move it

Tier: X/C synthesis of tier-A/L/B results already produced (E3-M0, E4-cosmic-web-tda). No new theory claim is made here beyond what those two scripts computed.

## 1. What E3 shows: the zero-parameter cosmological background is not rejected (a non-pre-registered comparison; consistent with Planck-LCDM, not a confirmation)

- M0 (0 theory parameters: Omega_Lambda frozen, GR tensor sector, symmetron sector deleted) matches DESI DR2 + the full Pantheon+SH0ES covariance (primary: 1580-SN cosmology-only cut) to Delta chi2 = 0.746 against a 1-parameter fit, 0.86 sigma (chi2.sf = 0.388); 9.00 is the 1-dof 3-sigma entry of the threshold table, not a pre-registered rule for this comparison (`audit/reverse_zero/E3-M0/m0_result.json`). That chi2 comparison, not AIC/BIC, is the load-bearing number; a negative Delta BIC here is a restatement that the frozen value happens to land near the data's optimum and would flip sign on a slightly worse miss, so it is reported as subordinate color, not independent confirmation.
- Reinstating mu_sym and c4_pta_product as free parameters (M2) buys **zero** fit improvement on any dataset in hand -- confirmed again on new DR2 + full-covariance data, reproducing round-3's finding. Both parameters remain, in the pre-registered language, "bounded, not predicted" (`PRE_REGISTRATION.md` P3), and today literally unbounded, since no fetched dataset tests either one (fifth-force table ABSENT; PTA angular table ABSENT).
- **This is the actual state of the "reduce to zero parameters" goal:** the background is already effectively zero-parameter and the data do not penalise that. The two nominal "free" parameters are not being fitted away by better theory -- they are simply untested. Zero-parameter status is currently achieved by *absence of a counter-example*, not by a derivation. That is a fragile kind of "zero".

## 2. What E4 shows: the TDA extension is a genuine new dataset, but it found no signal yet

- Extended TDA to real SDSS DR17 cosmic-web galaxy positions (193,536 galaxies, comoving 3D under the SAME frozen M0 background) vs. the paired random-shuffled-z null, PLUS a known-answer noisy-circle positive control run at the identical N so a null Betti tie cannot be confused with "the pipeline detected nothing" (`audit/reverse_zero/E4-cosmic-web-tda/cosmic_web_tda.py`, `e4_cosmic_web_tda_report.json`).
- At N=400 subsamples (seed 42), **Betti numbers are identical, [1,0], for both real and null**, while the circle control gives a clean dominant H1 bar (dominant-to-second ratio 29.7, comparable to round2/3's 62.9/49.05) -- confirming the pipeline CAN detect topology at this N. The real-vs-null tie is therefore attributable to the subsample, not pipeline insensitivity: at 400 galaxies drawn from 193,536 (~0.2%) over the survey footprint, the mean inter-galaxy separation in the subsample is far larger than the cosmic web's actual correlation length (a few Mpc/h), so random subsampling itself destroys the clustering geometry before any topology computation runs -- this is a subsampling-geometry effect, not a small-sample-statistics issue more repeats would fix, and not the round-3 "sparse-Rips artifact" (bottleneck distances were also checked against the known half-bar degeneracy bug and did not trigger it).
- **Honest read: this is a "gain" only in the sense of a new, real, previously-untried dataset now wired into the pipeline, with its diagnostics in place -- it did not yet find a distinguishing topology, and the reason is now specifically diagnosed (subsample size), not merely "underpowered".** Overclaiming a positive result here would repeat the round-1 mistake (a bounding-box TDA artifact later retracted).
- LeanMaster's octad/complement (E3, Stream 8) "8+16 split" has **no constructed observable** to compare against a galaxy catalogue; this script deliberately did not invent one. Building that map (e.g., partitioning by 24 angular/redshift bins and testing an 8-vs-16 asymmetry) is unbuilt and would be a new pre-registered hypothesis, per LeanMaster's own E4 note ("This would be a new hypothesis (H'), to be frozen before comparison. No such prediction is registered.").

## 3. Concrete, falsifiable next steps (ranked by what would most change the theory's status)

1. **Scale up E4's subsample and rerun with the round-3 diagnostic checklist** (multiple seeds, a matched-volume Poisson null in addition to the shuffled-z null, and the known-answer circle control) before drawing any conclusion about real vs. null topology. At N=400 the comparison is underpowered; the full 193,536-galaxy catalogue (or a well-chosen ~2000-5000 point subsample with a non-sparse or landmark-based Rips complex) is the natural next scale, budget permitting.
2. **Design and pre-register the octad-indexed statistic** before running it on data. This is the only path from LeanMaster's Tier-A/C E2-E4 mathematics (moduli-trapping selection, Golay 8+16 split, moonshine symmetry) to an actual falsifiable cosmological prediction; right now, per every LeanMaster consequence note quoted in this run, none of that mathematics touches a counted simulator parameter or a constructed observable. Skipping pre-registration here would repeat the round-4 "chosen after the fact, out of 4 variants" look-elsewhere mistake.
3. **Procure the two ABSENT decisive datasets** named across rounds 2-4 and this round: a machine-readable Eot-Wash alpha-lambda table (with a unit bridge for `mu_sym` -- currently there is none, and building one is a prerequisite, not optional), and a NANOGrav/IPTA angular Hellings-Downs correlation table with its bin-covariance (not the per-frequency KDE already in hand). Until at least one of these exists, `mu_sym` and `c4_pta_product` cannot be "reduced to zero" by data -- only by continuing to note that nothing tests them, which is a weaker claim than the pre-registration framework treats as a real reduction.
4. **Do not rescue P1/P2 by re-tuning kappa.** LeanMaster's kappa=1 result (Sec. 5 of `E3_RESULT.md`) closes that door formally: T-duality's own self-dual radius is a unique prediction, and it is excluded. The only bracket that survives is the lower half of [21,105] um coming from an unrelated Planck-mass convention ambiguity, and nothing in this round's results makes that survival stronger or weaker.

## 4. Bottom line

The zero-parameter *background* cosmology (M0) is not rejected by a non-pre-registered comparison (this round, on better data than before). The zero-parameter *sector* claims (no screening, no PTA excess) remain untested rather than confirmed, because the two decisive datasets are still ABSENT and the unit bridge for `mu_sym` does not exist. The new cosmic-web TDA data is real progress in coverage but, honestly reported, found no topology signal yet at the subsample size run here -- the "gain" is the dataset now being wired in, not a discovery. The single highest-leverage next action is #3 above (procure or bridge the two ABSENT datasets), because without them the "2 free parameters" in `REPORT.md`'s running count cannot become 0 by any means other than continuing to note their absence.
