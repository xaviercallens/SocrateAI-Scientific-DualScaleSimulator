## 6. Flux vacua (Track E) in the unimodular lattice

In the exact (not floating-point) Gram data for Γ_{3,19} = 3U ⊕ 2(−E8) and Γ_{3,3} = U^3: det = ±1, even diagonal on both, E8 positive-definite by Sylvester (E01a). Flux quantisation α=2λ with λ ∈ Γ (even lattice) forces α² mod 8 ∈ {0} for every one of 15625 sampled λ in the window; the odd unimodular control lattice I_{3,3} (realizable but ≠ H²(K3,ℤ)) gives {0,4} — the evenness, not an assumed 8, is what does the work (E01e2). With the TT (2.3) tadpole 24 (tier L) imposed, the admissible α_x² values in the even lattice are exactly {8,16,24} → N_D3 ∈ {16,8,0} (E03c); the odd control's admissible set is the larger {4,8,12,16,20,24} → N_D3 ∈ {0,4,8,12,16,20}, confirming evenness (not the number 24) drives the discreteness. The §4.1-family orbit count under O(Γ_{3,3}) is stable at (1,2,1) across windows N=1,2,3 for norms m=2,4,6 (E03d) — an INVARIANCE, not a rigidity claim. The §4.2 second branch (dim 1) has exactly 14 realizable (j,k) pairs with jk≤6 (E05a).

**What stays unfixed**: E05c/E06a quote TT directly (with line numbers) that the Kähler moduli are *not* completely determined — an 18-dimensional subspace of the K3 Kähler moduli space plus the T² Kähler modulus remain free even after the complex-structure moduli are stabilised by the flux. This is TT's own stated result, carried as a tier-L quotation, not re-derived.
